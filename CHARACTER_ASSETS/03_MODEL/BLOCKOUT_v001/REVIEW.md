# Blockout v001: Mila (CHR01_MILA) and Borek (CHR02_BOREK)

Character Lab internal check before audit submission. Built with Blender 5.2.3 LTS from `../scripts/blockout_v001.py`.
Measurements: `blockout_v001_measurements.json`. Total render time for every image here: 5 min 8 s on 4 CPU threads.

## What this stage is

Primary blockout: proportions, masses and silhouettes, built from simple primitives at real-world scale. Not yet production topology, sculpting, materials, a face rig or a body rig.

## Findings to fix in blockout v002

1. **Mila's side hair reads as two pigtails.** The smooth side masses look like a second braid on her left. The reference has wild, shoulder-length hair on both sides and one braid on her right. Make the side masses shorter, wider and broken up.
2. **Mila's hair is a smooth helmet.** The reference's signature is flyaway spikes. Add clumps that break the head outline in silhouette.
3. **Mila's shoes are too small** compared with the reference's chunky sneakers.
4. **Borek's crossed arms sit low and stick out sideways** like a bar. In the reference the arms are crossed higher and tighter, close under the chin, with the jacket bunched around the shoulders and no visible neck.
5. **Borek's apron is a full-width box.** In the reference it is narrower, hangs from a tie at the waist and lets the trousers show at the sides. Its colour family is right; it renders too light and will be tuned at look-dev.
6. **Staging B** needs Mila offset to the side so she isn't hidden.
7. **Borek's eyes are 6.8 px in mid-ground shots**, even smaller than AUD-001's estimate. That supports F3: his brows and mustache must carry his expressions.
