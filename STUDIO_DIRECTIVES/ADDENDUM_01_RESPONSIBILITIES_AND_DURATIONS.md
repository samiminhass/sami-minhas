# DIRECTIVE ADDENDUM 01 — DEPARTMENT RESPONSIBILITIES AND DURATION TIERS

Issued by: Executive Producer.
Extends: `SHORT_FORM_DISTRIBUTION_DIRECTIVE.md`. The shared directive sets the overall strategy; this addendum assigns clear responsibilities.

## 1. Additional department responsibilities

| Session | Department | Additional responsibility |
|---------|------------|---------------------------|
| 01 | Character Lab | Design characters, expressions, and assets that read clearly in vertical mobile videos. |
| 02 | Animation Studio | Produce 15–45-second vertical-first stories and three platform-ready deliverables. |
| 03 | Audit Studio | Audit short-form hooks, pacing, retention potential, sound, and technical quality before publishing. |

Session 03 must evaluate the **opening two seconds** of every short particularly carefully.

## 2. Initial publishing strategy: three duration tiers

Start with three distinct durations to discover what works.

### 15-second shorts: quick experiments

- One setup, one character reaction, one payoff.
- Best for simple comedy, unexpected actions, and visual surprises.

### 25–30-second shorts: core format

- Introduce a situation, develop a character interaction, and deliver an emotional or funny ending.
- Best initial format for testing the characters.

### 40–45-second shorts: mini-stories

- More character development, escalating situations, and stronger visual storytelling.
- Best for mini-adventures and small emotional stories.

The story still decides the length (shared directive, section 2). The tiers are for testing, not quotas.
