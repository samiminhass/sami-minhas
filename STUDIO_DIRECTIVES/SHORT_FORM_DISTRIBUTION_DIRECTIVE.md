# ADDITIONAL MASTER DIRECTIVE — SHORT-FORM ANIMATION & MULTI-PLATFORM DISTRIBUTION

Issued by: Executive Producer. Applies to all three studio departments.

## 1. OUR DISTRIBUTION OBJECTIVE

All completed animated productions will be published on our own newly established social media channels.

Our three primary platforms are:

1. YouTube — our official animation channel.
2. TikTok — our official short-form animation account.
3. Instagram — our official animation Reels account.

We are building an original animated entertainment brand.
These channels will feature our original characters, stories, environments, visual identity, and animation productions.
This is not a temporary experiment or a collection of unrelated animation uploads.
The long-term objective is to build a recognizable animated universe and an audience that returns to watch new stories.

## 2. INITIAL CONTENT STRATEGY

We are starting with short-form animated videos.

Target video duration:

* Minimum target: 15 seconds.
* Maximum target: 45 seconds.
* Initial standard: 20–30 seconds, subject to story requirements.

Every short must communicate a clear idea and deliver an engaging visual experience within its available duration.
Do not stretch a 15-second idea into 45 seconds.
Do not shorten a meaningful story simply to reach a particular duration.
Story quality and pacing take priority.

## 3. SHORT-FORM STORYTELLING STRUCTURE

Develop stories using a flexible structure:

**A. OPENING HOOK — First 0–2 seconds.**
Immediately give the viewer something worth watching.
Possible hooks:

* Unexpected character behavior.
* An intriguing visual situation.
* A surprising object or event.
* A funny expression.
* A mysterious moment.
* An unusual interaction.
* A problem already beginning.

Avoid unnecessary introductions, logos, title screens, or slow establishing sequences.

**B. STORY SETUP — Approximately 2–8 seconds.**
Communicate the situation through visuals, character behavior, and clear staging.
The viewer should understand what is happening without requiring a lengthy explanation.

**C. DEVELOPMENT — Approximately 8–25 seconds.**
Introduce:

* Character reactions.
* Conflict.
* Curiosity.
* Comedy.
* Escalation.
* Unexpected movement.
* Emotional development.

**D. PAYOFF — Final portion of the video.**
Deliver a satisfying outcome.
Possible endings:

* Unexpected comedy.
* An emotional moment.
* A surprising revelation.
* A clever visual twist.
* A heartwarming interaction.
* A memorable final expression.

These timings are guidelines, not mandatory formulas. Adapt them to the actual duration and story.

## 4. CONTENT FORMATS

Develop an original collection of recurring short-form animation formats.
Explore:

* **A. Silent Animated Stories** — Emotional stories communicated through gestures, expressions, movement, and visual storytelling.
* **B. Character Comedy** — Funny situations created through contrasting personalities, misunderstandings, reactions, and physical comedy.
* **C. Emotional Moments** — Short scenes involving affection, kindness, friendship, vulnerability, disappointment, and unexpected generosity.
* **D. Mini Adventures** — Small discoveries, unusual situations, environmental interactions, and unexpected challenges.
* **E. Character Relationships** — Recurring interactions that gradually establish personalities and relationships.
* **F. Mystery and Curiosity** — Short visual stories that introduce intriguing objects, events, and unexplained situations.
* **G. Recurring Series** — Develop recognizable formats, recurring locations, and connected storylines that encourage viewers to return.

Do not make every video follow the same plot structure.
Maintain variety while preserving the same creative identity.

## 5. VERTICAL-FIRST PRODUCTION

All initial short-form productions must be designed for vertical viewing.
Primary aspect ratio: 9:16.
Create appropriate vertical compositions in Blender.

Important production requirements:

* Characters must remain clearly visible on mobile screens.
* Facial reactions must be readable at small sizes.
* Important actions must remain within platform-safe areas.
* Do not place essential visual information behind interface overlays.
* Avoid unnecessarily wide character spacing.
* Use camera movements suitable for vertical storytelling.
* Preserve sufficient visual detail at mobile resolution.

Do not simply crop a horizontal animation into a vertical video.
Design the composition for vertical presentation from the storyboard stage.
Keep the original high-quality source project and render.
If we later produce horizontal YouTube episodes, create a separate 16:9 camera composition.

## 6. ONE PRODUCTION, THREE PLATFORM DELIVERABLES

The same approved story and animation can be distributed across all three platforms.
However, do not treat their publishing requirements as identical.
Prepare separate publishing packages:

**YOUTUBE:**

* YouTube Shorts version.
* Clear opening.
* Effective title.
* Appropriate description.
* Relevant channel identity.
* Suitable thumbnail or cover where supported.
* Accurate metadata.

**TIKTOK:**

* TikTok-ready vertical export.
* Native caption and description.
* Appropriate sound treatment.
* Relevant platform-native features where useful.
* An opening designed for immediate attention.

**INSTAGRAM:**

* Instagram Reels export.
* Strong cover frame.
* Appropriate caption.
* Consistent visual branding.
* Suitable account tagging and metadata.

Do not place another platform's watermark on our exports.
Keep an original clean master file for every production.
Verify the current technical and publishing requirements of each platform before final export.

## 7. SOUND AND MUSIC STRATEGY

Sound is a core part of our animation identity.
Develop:

* Original character sounds.
* Character-specific Foley.
* Environmental sound.
* Carefully selected music.
* Audio transitions.
* Silence at important moments.

Use original or properly licensed music and sound effects.
Do not assume that music available on one platform can legally be reused on another.
Prepare platform-specific audio versions where licensing or music availability requires it.
Sound must support the action and emotion rather than compete with the visuals.

## 8. BRAND IDENTITY

Develop a consistent animation-channel identity across YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram.
Create:

* Original channel name.
* Brand identity.
* Profile image.
* Channel banner.
* Visual theme.
* Typography.
* Color system.
* Channel description.
* Short-form cover design.
* Recurring visual elements.

Keep branding subtle within the actual animated stories.
Do not interrupt a story with unnecessary promotional graphics.
Characters and storytelling must remain the primary identity of the channel.

## 9. CONTENT PLANNING

Maintain a structured short-form content backlog.
For every proposed video document:

* Episode ID.
* Working title.
* Main character or characters.
* Story premise.
* Intended emotion.
* Opening hook.
* Story development.
* Visual payoff.
* Target duration.
* Production complexity.
* Required assets.
* Story status.
* Production status.
* Audit status.
* Publishing status.

Develop ideas in batches, but do not sacrifice quality for upload volume.
Prioritize repeatable production efficiency.

## 10. PUBLISHING AND CONSISTENCY

Build a sustainable publishing schedule.
The initial target is to produce a consistent supply of quality shorts while measuring the actual production capacity.
Recommend an upload cadence based on:

* Production time.
* Animation complexity.
* Review requirements.
* Rendering capacity.
* Available finished content.

Do not promise a daily upload schedule unless our production system can genuinely sustain it.
Prepare a content calendar for all three channels.
Each release should have its own publication record.
You must never claim that a video has been published unless the relevant platform has confirmed the upload.
Publishing and account-management actions require my approval.

## 11. AUDIENCE ANALYTICS AND CREATIVE IMPROVEMENT

After publishing, collect available analytics.

**YOUTUBE:** Viewed versus swiped away; engaged views; average view duration; average percentage viewed; audience retention; likes and comments; subscribers gained; shares.

**TIKTOK:** Video views; average watch time; completion rate; rewatches; shares; comments; follower conversion.

**INSTAGRAM:** Plays and views; watch time; average watch time; shares; saves; comments; reach; follows generated.

Compare results between similar video formats and durations.
Identify:

* Which openings hold attention.
* Which character interactions perform well.
* Which stories generate stronger emotional responses.
* Where viewers stop watching.
* Which visual styles are consistently effective.
* Which episodes encourage sharing and revisiting.

Do not treat total views as the only success metric.
Do not copy trending content simply because it performs well.
Use actual audience feedback to improve our original creative direction.

## 12. PRODUCTION QUALITY CONTROL

No video can bypass the independent audit.
Every short must be reviewed for:

* Story clarity.
* Opening strength.
* Character consistency.
* Facial performance.
* Animation mechanics.
* Camera composition.
* Lighting.
* Visual artifacts.
* Sound synchronization.
* Audio levels.
* Vertical framing.
* Export quality.

The short-form format does not justify lower animation standards.
A 15-second video can require excellent acting, timing, and direction.

## 13. FIRST-PHASE MILESTONES

* PHASE 1: Establish the channel identity and production workflow.
* PHASE 2: Complete the first approved characters.
* PHASE 3: Produce the first three pilot shorts.
* PHASE 4: Independently audit and refine the pilots.
* PHASE 5: Publish on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels.
* PHASE 6: Collect platform analytics and audience feedback.
* PHASE 7: Identify the strongest storytelling and animation formats.
* PHASE 8: Develop a repeatable short-form production system.
* PHASE 9: Expand into connected stories and longer YouTube productions when appropriate.

## FINAL DIRECTIVE

We are building an original short-form animated entertainment brand.
Every short must provide a reason to stop scrolling, a reason to continue watching, and a satisfying reason to remember the characters.

Our creative priorities are:

1. STORY FIRST.
2. CHARACTER ACTING SECOND.
3. VISUAL DIRECTION THIRD.
4. SOUND AND EDITING FOURTH.
5. DISTRIBUTION AND AUDIENCE LEARNING FIFTH.

Build a sustainable professional animation pipeline capable of producing original, high-quality short-form stories consistently.
