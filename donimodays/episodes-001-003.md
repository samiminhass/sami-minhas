# donimodays: First Three Episodes (current cast)

These replace the earlier Donimo episodes, which remain in this file's git history.
Each is a silent beat sheet for the Blender rigs. Control names are from
`blender/RIG_GUIDE.md`.

---

## Episode 1: Nix Touched It, "The Humming Shell"

- **Length:** about 25 seconds.
- **Title:** Don't touch the humming shell 🐚 #shorts

| # | Time | Shot | What happens | Main controls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0-3 s | Wide | A spiral dream shell lands softly and hums. Morrow's crest rises | `crest_mood` 1, `eye_target` on the shell |
| 2 | 3-7 s | Medium | Morrow leans, steps closer, and reaches with one hand | `body` lean, `foot_L/R` steps, `hand_IK` |
| 3 | 7-9 s | Insert | Nix darts in and taps the shell first. Its bead points at it | Nix `root` path, `attention`, `fin_A_flare` |
| 4 | 9-13 s | Close-up | The shell plays someone's dream: soft light on all three faces | Shell light only |
| 5 | 13-18 s | Medium | Vesper's fragments slowly gather. It is a dream Vesper knows | `frag_spread` -0.6, `brow_tilt` +0.6 |
| 6 | 18-22 s | Close-up | Nix realises everyone is looking at it and turns edge-on | Nix `root` turn, `lid_innocent` |
| 7 | 22-25 s | Wide, hold | Morrow hugs the shell. Vesper looks away, formally | `grip` 0.9, `head` turn |

## Episode 2: Vesper Keeps, "The Hundredth Try"

- **Length:** about 35 seconds.
- **Title:** It failed 99 times. Then this 🕯️ #shorts

| # | Time | Shot | What happens | Main controls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0-4 s | Wide | Morrow tries to climb a drifting stair of dream fragments and slips | `body`, feet, `crest_mood` -1 |

## Episode 3: The Lost Dream, "A Book Nobody Can Read"

- **Length:** about 40 seconds. Based on a real mystery, the Voynich Manuscript.

**Facts to check before publishing.**
- Carbon dating places its parchment in the early 1400s.
- It is written in an unknown script that no one has convincingly decoded.
- It is named after Wilfrid Voynich, who bought it in 1912.
- It is kept at Yale University's Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library.

| # | Time | Shot | What happens | Main controls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0-4 s | Wide | A heavy old book drifts down and lands open. Strange writing glows faintly | Book light only |
| 2 | 4-10 s | Insert | Plant drawings unfold off the pages. Caption: its pages date from the early 1400s | |
| 3 | 10-16 s | Medium | Morrow tries to read it, tilting its whole body. Nothing makes sense | `body` tilt, `brow_worry`, `eye_target` |
| 4 | 16-22 s | Close-up | Nix's bead races across the lines, then stops, confused | `attention` |
| 5 | 22-30 s | Medium | Vesper leans in, then slowly closes the book with great formality. Caption: nobody has ever decoded it | `spine`, `hand_IK`, `grip` |
| 6 | 30-36 s | Close-up | Morrow looks at the book, then at the viewer | `eye_target` to camera |
| 7 | 36-40 s | Wide, hold | Caption: whose dream was it? Comment below | Held pose |
